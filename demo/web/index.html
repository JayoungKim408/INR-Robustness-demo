Attacking INR classifiers with a hypernetwork surrogate

MNIST · victims: DWS and ScaleGMN · image-space L∞ attack, -step PGD, best of restarts

These classifiers don't see pixels; they classify a SIREN (INR) fitted to the image. The attacker only edits the image:
  1. A hypernetwork maps an image to SIREN weights, so image → hypernet → victim is differentiable.
  2. PGD on that chain gives an adversarial image (only the image changes).
  3. A fresh SIREN is fitted to the adversarial image and the real victim classifies it.
